# Demo listing photo credits

The demo listings created by `npm run db:seed` use one photo per catalogue item. All photos come from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) under free licences (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA)
and were resized to at most 960 px wide. They are stored on our Cloudinary account in `<CLOUDINARY_FOLDER>/demo/`
(file name = the slug in `DEMO_PHOTO` in `src/scripts/seed.js`).

They are **representative** photos of the kind of item, not photos of the actual items. The textbooks use generic
book photos because real cover photos are copyrighted. Listings that students create show their own uploaded photos.

CC BY and CC BY-SA licences require crediting the author: keep this file with the project, and link to it
(for example from the site footer) if the demo listings are shown publicly.
